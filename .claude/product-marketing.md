# Gamify product marketing context

Read by the vendored marketing skills (`.claude/skills/social`, `copywriting`,
`video` and the rest). The source of truth is `docs/marketing-bible.md`; if
anything here disagrees with it, the bible wins.

- **Product**: Gamify, a K-12 learning game platform from Vermont. Teachers
  turn their own question sets into classroom games, live with a code or link
  or as homework. Students join only through their teacher; no student accounts
  with email.
- **Buyers**: K-12 teachers, homeschool parents and co-ops, tutors, and school
  or district leaders (bible section 3).
- **Goal**: paid subscriptions. Plans: Free; Plus $8 a month; Pro $15 a month
  (3, 6 and 12-month plans cost less); Homeschool; school and district plans.
- **Voice**: warm, plain, teacher-to-teacher, short sentences (bible section 4).
  Words to use and avoid: bible section 4.
- **Proof**: only true claims about what ships; research only as worded in
  `docs/gamified-learning-research.md`; no test-score promises.
- **Never**: another company's name or brand; student faces, names, voices or
  work without a signed release; content aimed at children; ads to under-18s
  (bible section 7).
- **Channels**: Instagram and TikTok first, then Facebook teacher groups and
  Pinterest. Affiliates: `docs/affiliate-program.md`.
- **Approval**: the owner approves every post, ad, boost and dollar.
