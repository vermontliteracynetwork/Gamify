# Demo video plan (launch readiness, Stage 8)

Two videos for the public site and for emails to schools. A silent rough cut
recorded from the real app (2026-10-25, about 80 seconds) shows the flow and
pacing; the final versions add voice, captions and classroom footage with
signed releases.

## Video 1: "Every question becomes a game" (60-90 seconds, home page)

| Time | On screen | Voice-over (plain, warm, one teacher talking to another) |
|---|---|---|
| 0-8 s | Home page, covers lift as the cursor moves | "You already have the questions. Gamify turns them into games your class will beg to play." |
| 8-20 s | Make a set or pick a ready-made one; filter by a standard code | "Type your own questions, import a spreadsheet, or start from a ready-made set." |
| 20-40 s | Game Show on a projector: a team picks a card, taps the answer, the card turns over | "Play together on one big screen, with teams or the whole class against the computer." |
| 40-55 s | A student on an iPad: Bubble Pop, aiming and popping; read-aloud button | "Or every student plays on their own device, with read-aloud and supports built in." |
| 55-70 s | Theme Builder: a class drawing photographed and dropped into the game | "Your class can even design the look of the game." |
| 70-85 s | Reports, then the Join and play for free button | "Every answer lands in your reports. Join and play for free." |

## Video 2: "Your first ten minutes" (3 minutes, help page and welcome email)

1. Log in, make a class, paste the class list.
2. Assign a set; show the join code on the projector.
3. A student joins with the code and picture password.
4. Play one Share game and one Solo game.
5. Open Reports and Let's Look Back.

## Rules for every version

- Captions on every line (burned in and as a caption file), read-aloud
  friendly pacing.
- No student faces or names without a signed release; use first-name-only
  sample students (Ava, Ben) in screen recordings.
- No other company's brand, logo or game on screen, including browser tabs
  and bookmarks: record in a clean browser profile.
- Music: none, or a track with a commercial license logged in
  `docs/asset-ledger.md`. Gamify's own sounds are code-made and free to use.
- Claims follow the Research page wording: "studies found", never "proven".
- Record at 1280 x 720 or higher, the iPad parts at iPad size.

## Rough cut

`scripts/record-demo.mjs` records the walkthrough automatically from the app (run the
preview server, then `node scripts/record-demo.mjs <folder>`), so the rough cut can be
re-recorded after every design change. Waiting on you: voice-over, classroom
footage with releases, and approval of the final cut.
