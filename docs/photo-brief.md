# Photo brief: classrooms playing and making themes

Owner, 2026-11-27: "these are some examples of pictures i want of students
playing our games in various ways and also creating the themes artistically.
these are the kind of photos i want in the carosel and promotional otherwise.
the beta test will have some requirements and we will abide by photo releases
for faces or if it needs to be hands/back of head/work only."

The 13 example photos she sent are **references only**. They were found online,
so none of them ships, in the app, the site or any post (legal rule: no "found
online" assets). Real photos come from beta classrooms, with releases.

## Shot list (from her examples)

Playing, in every way Gamify is played:
1. Two or three kids at a smart board, one tapping or pointing (the Share way).
2. A whole class at desks with laptops, the game on the big screen behind them.
3. Small groups on Chromebooks or iPads, headphones on, leaning in.
4. Kids on the carpet facing the projector, a few hands up.
5. One student close up on a laptop or tablet (Solo or homework), screen visible.
6. A pair side by side at one screen (Versus).
7. The teacher beside a student, pointing at the screen.

Making themes, artistically:
8. Kids drawing pieces with crayons, markers and paint at a shared table.
9. A group around one big sheet, pencil cases open.
10. A finished drawing held up next to the game that uses it.
11. Teachers at a table making a theme together (PD day), laptop open.

## Release rules (each photo is tagged in `src/public/ThemeGallery.tsx`)

| Tag | What it may show | What we need on file |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `faces` | Faces of children or adults | A signed photo release for every person shown (parent or guardian for children), naming web, social and ads use |
| `hands` | Hands, arms, the back of a head, no face | The school's OK to photograph and the teacher's OK |
| `work` | Only the drawings, the screen or the room, no people | The teacher's OK |

Also: no names, name tags, school logos, student screens with full names, or
anything that places a child. Never on student pages. Keep each release with
the photo's file name in the beta folder; add each photo to
`docs/asset-ledger.md` with its tag before it ships.

## Where they go

- Pricing page, "Made by your class" carousel (four slides: draw it, snap it
  in, play it, pick the next one). Each slide shows its drawn stand-in until a
  released photo is added to `GALLERY`.
- Home, Features, Beta pages and social posts once there are enough.
