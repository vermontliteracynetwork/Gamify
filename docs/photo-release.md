## Part A. For the parent or guardian (photos that may show a child's face)

**Gamify** ([legal business name, to confirm]), a classroom game website, would
like to take or receive photos and short videos of your child playing Gamify
games or drawing game art in class. We use them to show teachers what Gamify
looks like in a real classroom.

**Student:** ______________________ (first name only, for our records)
**School and teacher:** ______________________

### Please choose one

- [ ] **Yes, my child's face may appear** in Gamify photos and videos.
- [ ] **Hands and work only.** My child may appear only as hands, arms or the
      back of the head, or only their drawings and screen may be shown. No face.
- [ ] **No.** My child must not appear in any Gamify photo or video.

### Where the photos may be used
- [ ] The Gamify website and the teacher pages of the Gamify app
- [ ] Gamify social media (Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, TikTok)
- [ ] Gamify ads (online ads on those platforms and search ads)
- [ ] Printed material (flyers, conference posters)

### Our promises
1. **No names.** We never print your child's name, last name, school name,
   name tag, grade or anything that shows where they are. We crop or blur
   screens that show names.
2. **No student pages.** Photos never appear on pages students use.
3. **Never sold.** We do not sell or license the photos to anyone else, and
   we do not use them to train artificial intelligence.
4. **No payment.** There is no payment for taking part, and saying no changes
   nothing about your child's class or how they use Gamify.
5. **You can change your mind.** Email [contact email, to confirm] or tell the
   teacher. We remove the photos from our website and social accounts within
   [10, to confirm] business days and stop using them in new material. Posts
   other people already shared and printed material already handed out may not
   be recoverable.
6. **How long.** We use the photos for up to [3 years, to confirm] from the
   date below, then stop using them in new material.
7. **Kept safely.** Photos are stored in a private folder only the Gamify team
   can open, with this form kept beside them.
