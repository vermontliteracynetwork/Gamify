## Takeaways for Gamify

- **Fair fun is the biggest gap.** Stealing and luck are the loudest complaint on two platforms (tears in K-2, "brings out the worst"). Keep steal-style power-ups off by default and free to switch on (already Gamify's rule), and make right answers decide the outcome.
- **Free, live, per-question teacher view.** Teachers pay elsewhere for question-level reports and cannot see whole-class answers live. Gamify's reports and live monitor answer this; lead sales with it.
- **One screen and own devices, both.** Teachers love no-device, no-login team play for K-2; Gamify does that and still keeps per-student data.
- **Collecting is the hook, tied to learning.** Kids love unlocks; Gamify's stickers come from playing and trying, not speed or buying.
- **Trust sells.** Name pick from the class list (no nickname drama), cheat-resistant lobbies, clear billing reminders and easy cancel, keep editing after cancelling, no ads, checked templates.
- **Fast setup and a fresh look.** Teachers love minutes-to-build; they call dated looks "old school".
