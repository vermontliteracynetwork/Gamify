# Teacher preferences: apply without being asked

Standing rule (teacher, 2026-10-31): "i should not have to continue to repeat
myself over the same things that Claudia could just apply as what i would want
based on my past requests." Read this before building anything, apply every
line that fits, and add each new preference the same turn she says it.

## Games: look and feel
- The Golden Rule (.claude/skills/golden-rule) governs all game design.
- Video-game quality, held to popular classroom games and mobile hits; never "it works, ship it".
- Match her reference images and her exact words; use the assets she uploads first.
- Boards fill the whole purple area; small round controls (Full screen, Accessibility tools, Settings) overlap at the top right; full screen looks the same.
- Never any scrolling on a game screen; boards size to the screen on iPad (both ways), laptop, smart board and big desktop.
- Scoreboard right beside the board, top aligned; the current player glows; no extra banners or step bars.
- No numbers on board spaces unless she asks; show with shape, color, icons and motion.
- Every move animated (hops, rolls, bounces); nothing teleports; motion has weight and some randomness.
- Unpredictability and irregularity: varied outcomes, wobble, lucky breaks, comebacks.
- Power-ups frequent and wacky, each with its own animation, art and plain role ("2x points: Earn double points"); held ones wait for the player's next turn under the scoreboard in a vertical list and vanish after that turn.
- Negative power-ups land at once, are funny not mean; Steal/Swap style takes start off.
- Board Game players are the 20 voxel animals; students pick theirs.
- Turn card before every team question with the player's own icon; tap to start. Only the icon, no "it's your turn" words, and it comes before the move: tap it, answer, then move if right, following the tries setting (2026-11-01).
- All cards and tiles the same size; perfect circles stay circles; outlines never cut off.
- Every pop-up has an X; results open in a box over the game (winner on a stage with a halo, Grow Year title, Play again, New game (teacher, 2026-11-01), Review, Rate; never an "Arcade" button).
- Classic is the default theme of every game and is never handed out; designed themes come from her packs.
- Start menus (welcome, theme, setup) and results always wear the standard Gamify look.
- Setup menus NEVER scroll: settings that do not fit flow onto another page (teacher, 2026-10-31).
- Say "rounds" (not "question breaks"); Bubble Pop: 10 rounds by default (3 to 20), 3 shots each round (1 to 5).
- Games must have skill plus surprise: aim, spin or timing the player controls, and physics or chance that makes every turn different.
- Setup in plain words with defaults ("Moves before a question break, default 3"); every step and setting clearly shown.
- Every Share game also plays Versus (one device, two players or the computer).

- Game Show cards are always horizontal (wider than tall), whatever the grid size (2026-11-01).
- A game's cover image always mirrors how the game and its theme really look (2026-11-01).
- Every game uses the same question set picker: square cards that fill whole rows (4, 5 or 6 across, whatever fits) and never cut off a row, no cover pictures, no "Pick a question set" line (2026-11-01).
- Power-up choices: every power-up on one page as small square cards; tap a card to turn it on or off (2026-11-01).
- Every game lets the teacher switch each power-up on or off, like Game Show's list (2026-11-01).
- A game's background color can be changed per game; Game Show's default is red (2026-11-01).
- A power-up that takes, steals or swaps points never comes up when the players it would hit have no points (all games, 2026-11-01).
- Everything moves slowly enough to follow: computer turns, menus, pop-ups and play (2026-11-01).

## Platform
- Brand look: periwinkle ground, off-white panels, black outlines, pale yellow accent, salmon, coral, navy, mint; Grow Year for titles, Inclusive Sans for everything else (no Loyola).
- Students reach games only by a code or link; class students tap their name.
- "Activity" is the word for game + set + who plays.
- English only; no em dashes; no other brands in customer text; no ads or upgrade prompts on student pages; accessibility never paywalled.
- Every page opens at the top; breadcrumbs to go back.
- Every asset licensed and in docs/asset-ledger.md before it ships.

## Assets she sends
- Packs she uploads from itch.io come with commercial licenses (teacher, 2026-11-01: "all found on itch.io with commercial licensing"). Record them in the asset ledger as confirmed by her and use them; don't hold them in Waiting on you. Still ask for the license line when a pack needs credit (CC BY) or forbids something.

## Working style
- Keep the queue running; predict her answers from past decisions and log the guess.
- Quiet build: no narration; short final report starting with Waiting on you.
- Test in a real browser at every size before calling anything done; read console errors.
- Run the golden-rule skill and a Claudia review on every game change.
- Bowling balls: each ball is a round template piece; up to 10 can be drawn; a player's icon is the ball they pick (2026-11-01).
- Uploading pieces: no separate "add photos of the pages" button; every piece's upload takes a photo of its page or of a drawing and cuts the piece out (2026-11-01).
- Every upload is cut to the piece's exact size; the system tells a hand-drawn template photo (corner squares) from digital art (2026-11-01).
- The Theme Builder preview is a game preview with tabs for each screen the pieces show on (Bowling: Lane, Pick a ball, Power-ups) (2026-11-01).
- Predicted (2026-11-01, market gaps): question sets get true or false in one tap, the teacher's own recorded voice for a question, and an optional teach card read aloud before play; all free on every plan.
- Board Game: the whole stage is the board (countryside all around); panels float over the countryside, never over the trail; power-up spaces are hidden and random; no blank tiles (2026-11-01).
- Turn cards: just the player's picture, no circle, no box, no hand; a soft glow and a bob (2026-11-01).
- Players with characters (Board Game animals) show as player cards with the animal big and no circle; the question header shows the player's animal (2026-11-01).
- Teacher-supplied sounds: right and wrong answers everywhere; dice shake for every roll; jump on every hop; magnet, tornado, chest power-ups; Pinball bumper, score bell and flipper sounds; Cast and Catch cast, splash and bells (2026-11-01).
- Shaped pieces get shaped template outlines and cut along them (Bowling pin first); drawn pins always show inside the pin outline in the game (2026-11-02).
- Theme Builder preview, Pick a ball: tap a ball to see it big on white; tap again to go back (2026-11-02).
- Draw studio: Restore (magic wand) rubs back what the eraser took off the last upload; Start over returns to that upload; a new upload always replaces old drawing (2026-11-02).
- Answer sounds everywhere: an up chime for right, a boing for wrong or try again; never a harsh buzzer (2026-11-05).
- Menus are full views, never small cards (2026-11-06): game welcome, setup steps and Play or share fill the whole space; covers always show whole, never cropped.
- Ways to play show as just an icon in a colored circle; the hover text names it in the same color (2026-11-06).
- Game Show category grid: show example category names and explain it as a classic TV quiz-show board, without naming any show (2026-11-06).
- Set pickers: small search and filters, cards are the main view; My sets and Ready-made sets tabs (2026-11-06).
- Options are playful: a highlight that slides to the pick with a little bounce; grid sizes show a tiny grid picture; power-up cards show their description on the card, not in hover text (2026-11-06).
- Versus players are big tall cards with the icon large (2026-11-06).
