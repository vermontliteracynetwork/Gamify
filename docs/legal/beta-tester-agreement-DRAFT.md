# Gamify Beta Tester Agreement (DRAFT for attorney review)

Draft written 2026-10-25 from the dev plan (Part 5A, What beta testing
Gamify involves). Not legal advice; the attorney must review before any
teacher sees it. Plain language on purpose: teachers should be able to read
it in two minutes.

## What you get

- Gamify Plus free for the beta period (3 months with the Vermont Literacy
  Network code, or as long as Gamify chooses for invited beta teachers).
- Early access to new games and features, and a direct line to the people
  building Gamify.
- Your name (only if you say yes) on the founding teachers page.

## What we ask

1. **Set up a class:** create a classroom, add the class list, and assign
   at least one question set.
2. **Teach the Tell Gamify button:** a short lesson (about 10 minutes) using
   the beta classroom kit (gamify, /beta-kit), so students can report bugs
   and ideas without typing names.
3. **Play regularly:** at least 2 times a week during the beta.
4. **Quick student check-ins:** the anonymous picture check-in after games,
   when it fits your day.
5. **Teacher surveys:** about 5 minutes each at week 1, month 1 and month 3.
6. **Report problems as they happen** with the Tell Gamify button.
7. **Optional:** classroom photos or quotes (only with signed releases) and
   a 15-minute call at the end.

## Student privacy (our promise)

- Students use a first name or nickname and picture passwords. No student
  emails, no ads, no trackers, nothing sold.
- Check-ins are anonymous: only the counts are kept.
- You can download or delete your class data at any time.
- Beta feedback is used only to improve Gamify. We never publish anything
  that identifies a student.
- Your school's own rules about classroom technology still apply; please
  check with your school before using Gamify with students.

## The beta is a beta

- Things will break sometimes. Please keep a backup plan for any lesson
  that depends on Gamify.
- Features, games and prices may change before launch. Anything you build
  (question sets, classes, themes) stays yours and can be exported.
- Either side can end the beta at any time. If you stop, your account moves
  to the Free plan and nothing is deleted.

## Feedback you send

You give Gamify permission to use your feedback and ideas to improve the
product, without payment. We will not use your name or your school's name
in marketing without asking you first, in writing.

## For the attorney to decide

- Whether a school or district must also agree (some districts require a
  data privacy agreement before any classroom tool is used).
- COPPA and FERPA wording: Gamify collects minimal student data under the
  school's authority; confirm the right "school official" language.
- Vermont-specific student privacy law (Vermont's Student Privacy Act) and
  any state data privacy agreement template schools ask vendors to sign.
- Liability limits for a free beta.
