# Lane Hop assets

Everything in the Classic theme is drawn in code (`Game.tsx`): the hopper,
every lane (meadow, hay field, river, lily pads, gravel, train tracks, snow,
ice, moon rock, space), hay bales, logs, the train and its warning light,
penguins, asteroids, stars, power-up pictures and the cover. No image files.

| Phosphor icons (buttons, How to play) | @phosphor-icons/react | MIT, in `docs/asset-ledger.md` |
